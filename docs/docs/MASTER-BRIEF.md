# Master Brief

Status: **Source of truth / locked unless a documented technical, legal, or user-validated reason requires change**

Last updated: 2026-08-22

## Product definition

**ONLEV** (`onlev.site`) is a premium digital-systems company whose marketing experience contains the Three Doors live showroom for complete, industry-specific client-acquisition systems. It is not an agency portfolio, a template marketplace, or a collection of decorative concept sites.

The initial product serves three lead-driven local-business categories:

1. Real-estate agents and teams
2. Plumbers and home-service businesses
3. Personal-injury and accident lawyers

The delivered product is broader than a website. Each system may include positioning, brand direction, conversion copy, responsive implementation, useful customer tools, lead qualification, CRM routing, call/form/booking attribution, automated follow-up, analytics, local-search foundations, accessibility, performance, security, hosting, and iteration.

## Business objective

Make a qualified business owner say: **“This understands how my customers choose, and I want this system for my business.”**

The showroom must prove:

- Industry understanding
- Client-ready design quality
- Practical usefulness
- Conversion thinking
- Technical credibility
- A clear path from visitor intent to business follow-up

The primary conversion is a personalized walkthrough or fit conversation—not a generic newsletter signup.

## Primary audiences

### Buyer

An owner, operator, partner, or growth-minded leader at a local business. They may not be technically sophisticated. They care about trust, differentiation, lead quality, speed to response, measurable return, and whether the system feels appropriate for their market.

### Buyer’s customer

The showroom must also satisfy the end users represented inside each demo:

- A buyer or seller making a financially significant decision
- A homeowner handling an urgent or complex plumbing problem
- An injured person seeking clarity during a stressful period

## ONLEV identity

The approved ONLEV mark is a square architectural frame containing the compact ON / LEV construction, with the final V expressed as a controlled warm-metal beam. The production identity preserves this composition across navigation, responsive, transparent, light, dark, and app-icon uses.

The identity does not turn the entire experience into a black-and-gold theme. Warm metal is concentrated in the mark and selected material moments; the parent interface remains the locked mineral-neutral showroom system with signal blue reserved for decisive action and system flow.

The Three Doors remains the signature showroom concept inside ONLEV. The parent company name replaces the former working product name in public-facing brand surfaces without redesigning the completed Hero or Industry Hub.

## Investment position

ONLEV is a premium custom engagement, not a set of low-cost page tiers. Scope and investment are defined after a fit conversation and discovery because they change with content, custom tools, locations, data, integrations, compliance, production media, and operational readiness. Do not invent a starting price, ROI claim, client result, or false package boundary before those inputs are known.

## Customer journey

The intended emotional and functional sequence is:

1. **Recognition:** understand the offer within five seconds.
2. **Self-selection:** choose Real Estate, Home Services, or Injury Law.
3. **Hands-on proof:** enter a believable, functioning demo—not a video mockup.
4. **Practical value:** use a tool and receive a useful result before being asked for contact details where practical.
5. **System understanding:** activate the System Lens to see trust, qualification, routing, follow-up, and attribution beneath the experience.
6. **Confidence:** review process, integrations, performance, accessibility, and proof.
7. **Ownership:** picture the system with the visitor’s brand, territory, services, team, and tools.
8. **Conversion:** request a personalized walkthrough.

## Locked creative direction

### Signature concept: The Three Doors

The master experience is an architectural, gallery-like structure with three illuminated apertures representing Homes, Home Services, and Injury Law. Each aperture previews a real customer interaction and business outcome. The hero evolves into the industry hub; they are not separate concepts.

Selecting a door creates a short, interruptible spatial transition into an independently routed demo website. The same destination must also be available through ordinary semantic links and a reduced-motion crossfade.

### System Lens

Every demo will ultimately support:

- **Customer view:** the believable public-facing experience
- **System Lens:** an owner-facing explanatory layer showing the acquisition machinery beneath it

The System Lens is a product demonstration, not a developer-debug overlay.

## Experience principles

- 3D must serve the sales narrative.
- One authored cinematic moment is stronger than constant animation.
- Essential copy and navigation always exist in semantic HTML.
- The experience must remain persuasive with WebGL disabled.
- Mobile receives an authored alternative, not a compressed desktop scene.
- Tools become visually quiet and stable when the user is entering information.
- Each demo must feel independently commissioned, not like a reskin.
- Useful outputs come before lead capture when practical.
- No fake awards, reviews, rankings, results, listings, testimonials, or case values.
- No mandatory intro, long loader, scroll trap, cursor takeover, or hidden navigation.

## Trauma-aware law direction

The cracked-windshield concept is not the parent hero. Injury-law imagery may use abstract fractured light resolving into order and clarity, but must avoid graphic impact, sirens, blood, first-person crash reenactment, or forced sensory intensity.

The law tool provides urgency and next-step guidance—not settlement estimates, legal conclusions, or representation guarantees. Full date of birth is deferred to a secure secondary intake only if attorney review establishes a legitimate need.

## Future marketplace boundary

The codebase should make a fourth vertical possible through a clean registry and shared infrastructure. It must not yet implement creator accounts, multi-tenancy, billing, template editing, publishing workflows, or a general site builder. Those decisions wait until the showroom proves demand.

## Phase boundaries

- Phase 2 establishes documentation, tokens, primitives, dependencies, and architecture.
- No full public page, Three Doors hero, industry hub, or demo is part of Phase 2.
- Phase 3 should build the first semantic showroom shell and a bounded Three Doors feasibility prototype.

## Decision-change protocol

A locked decision may change only when at least one of these is true:

- A measured performance or accessibility problem cannot be resolved within the decision.
- Legal or professional review requires a change.
- User testing disproves the underlying assumption.
- The product owner explicitly changes the business direction.

Record any change in `CURRENT-STATE.md` with the reason, impact, and affected documents before implementation.

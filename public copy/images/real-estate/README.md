# Real Estate image provenance

These six raster assets were generated for this project on 2026-08-22 with the
workspace image-generation tool. They are fictional editorial imagery and do
not depict listed properties, actual clients, or a real agent.

- `foothill-house.jpg` — cinematic dusk exterior of a fictional modern foothill residence
- `foothill-interior.jpg` — quiet editorial interior of the same fictional residence
- `spanish-courtyard.jpg` — fictional Spanish Colonial courtyard house
- `craftsman-oaks.jpg` — fictional Craftsman residence under mature oaks
- `pasadena-streetscape.jpg` — objective architectural neighborhood streetscape
- `elena-ward.jpg` — fictional real-estate advisor portrait

Generation direction emphasized natural architectural photography, restrained
color, believable materials, editorial framing, and the absence of logos,
signage, text, watermarks, or identifiable real-world properties. The delivered
PNGs were converted locally to optimized JPEGs without compositing third-party
material.

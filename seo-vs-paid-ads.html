FAQ

How should a business use this?

Use it as an audit checklist. Identify weak points, fix the most obvious conversion leaks first, then build a consistent monthly system around the improvements.

What should be avoided?

Avoid unsupported claims, fake urgency, copied content, disconnected ad campaigns, and pages that look visually expensive but fail to explain the offer clearly.
